Abstract: Satellite remote sensing images of the Lijiang karst area suffer from severe object fragmentation, substantial scale variations, and spectral ambiguity. Traditional segmentation algorithms struggle to preserve fine landform details and global semantic cues, and generally fail to balance segmentation accuracy with model efficiency. To address these challenges, we propose a Bidirectional Multi‑scale Mask‑aware Bilateral Awareness Network (BMBANet), which builds upon the Bilateral Awareness Network (BANet). Specifically, we construct a Bidirectional Multi‑scale Collaborative Extraction Mechanism (BM‑CEM) to achieve bidirectional multi‑level feature fusion, enhancing the model's adaptability to multi‑scale objects. We also design an improved Mixed Pooling block (PriMixPool), which integrates prior masks with a two‑dimensional attention mechanism to effectively suppress image noise and refine the boundaries of fragmented land cover. Furthermore, we build a Lijiang National Nature Reserve dataset based on Sentinel‑2 imagery and extensively validate our method on both this dataset and the public WHU‑OPT‑SAR benchmark. Experimental results demonstrate that our model achieves superior segmentation performance with only 12.6M parameters. On our dataset, it attains an mIoU of 65.0%, outperforming the baseline BANet by 3.8%. Our approach significantly improves the segmentation of challenging karst classes—especially roads and bareland—while maintaining a low computational cost. These strengths make it a reliable and efficient tool for fine‑scale ecological monitoring in the Lijiang karst region.
